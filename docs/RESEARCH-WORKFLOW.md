# End-to-End Product R&D Workflow

## Stage 1. Frame the decision
Use [Research Brief](../templates/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md). Specify product, customer, objective, scope, timeline, and success criteria.

## Stage 2. Select research modules
Use [categories 1 to 180](../research/RECONSTRUCTED-CATEGORIES-001-180.md), [categories 181 to 275](../research/DETAILED-CATEGORIES-181-275.md), and [industry modules](../research/INDUSTRY-MODULES.md). Choose only relevant modules.

## Stage 3. Gather evidence
Use [Source Register](../templates/SOURCE-REGISTER.md). Separate primary observations from third-party reports and assumptions.

## Stage 4. Size the opportunity
Use [TAM/SAM/SOM guide](../market/TAM-SAM-SOM.md). Triangulate estimates and run sensitivity analysis.

## Stage 5. Test the biggest unknown
Use [Experiment Card](../templates/EXPERIMENT-CARD.md). Never report planned tests as completed.

## Stage 6. Review risk and options
Use [Risk Register](../templates/RISK-REGISTER.md) and [Decision Log](../templates/DECISION-LOG.md). Challenge assumptions and examine alternatives.

## Stage 7. Deliver
Use [Product R&D Report](../templates/PRODUCT-RD-REPORT.md). Present decision, evidence, trade-offs, confidence, roadmap, and next actions.

## Stage 8. Revisit
Define metrics and conditions that trigger reconsideration. Stop researching when expected decision value is low.

## Depth modes
- **Rapid:** Decision brief, key sources, critical unknowns, three options, immediate next test.
- **Standard:** Customer and competitor research, opportunity sizing, risk register, experiment plan, roadmap.
- **Deep:** Multiple methods, detailed technical and financial models, independent critique, replication.
- **Exhaustive:** All decision-relevant disciplines, domain specialist review, auditable evidence and experiment records. Exhaustive does not mean every category is mandatory.

# Product R&D Metrics and Strategy Frameworks

## Market and validation
TAM, SAM, SOM, CAGR, market share, penetration, saturation, concentration, fragmentation, ICP, JTBD, PMF, problem-solution fit, willingness to pay (WTP), voice of customer (VOC), value proposition, concept tests, prototype tests, MVP, minimum lovable product.

## Revenue and economics
CAC, LTV/CLV, ARPU, ARPA, MRR, ARR, ACV, TCV, net revenue retention (NRR), gross revenue retention (GRR), customer and revenue churn, gross margin, contribution margin, CAC payback, burn, runway, ROI, ROIC, NPV, IRR, total cost of ownership (TCO).

## Product engagement
AARRR, DAU, WAU, MAU, DAU/MAU, activation, retention, conversion, time to value, north star metric, NPS, CSAT, customer effort score, feature adoption, funnel analysis, cohort analysis.

## Strategy and prioritization
SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces, VRIO, Ansoff Matrix, BCG Matrix, Blue Ocean Strategy, value chain, Business Model Canvas, Value Proposition Canvas, RICE, ICE, MoSCoW, WSJF, Opportunity Solution Tree.

## Useful formulas
| Metric | Formula | Important caveat |
|---|---|---|
| CAGR | `(End / Start)^(1 / Years) - 1` | Same measurement basis |
| Market share | `Company market sales / Total market sales × 100` | Comparable market scope |
| CAC | `Acquisition costs / New customers` | Consistent cohorts |
| ARPU | `Revenue / Average users` | Define period |
| Gross margin | `(Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100` | Consistent COGS |
| Conversion | `Conversions / Eligible population × 100` | Define denominator |
| Retention | `Original cohort retained / Original cohort × 100` | Do not include new customers |
| ROI | `(Investment gain - Cost) / Cost × 100` | State included costs |

## Research methods
Conjoint analysis, Kano model, Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger, A/B testing, cohort studies, causal inference, usability tests, ethnography, diary studies, interviews, and controlled benchmarking. Survey-based pricing methods measure stated preferences, not guaranteed purchase behavior.

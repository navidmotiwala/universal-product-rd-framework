# Universal Product R&D Framework

A practical, product-agnostic research and development toolkit for investigating, validating, improving, building, and commercializing products.

**Scope:** 275 reference research categories, 20 industry overlays, a 20-phase workflow, market sizing, product economics, and reusable prompts.

> The category count is a reference index, not proof of exhaustive coverage. Select research relevant to the product and decision.

## How to use

1. Open the [original 35-section master prompt](prompts/UNIVERSAL-PRODUCT-RD-MASTER-PROMPT-ORIGINAL.md) or the [expanded 20-phase version](prompts/UNIVERSAL-RD-MASTER-PROMPT.md).
2. Enter a product name or URL and the research objective.
3. Choose rapid, standard, deep, or exhaustive research.
4. Select relevant modules, not all categories.
5. Collect evidence and record assumptions.
6. Test the uncertainties that could change decisions.
7. Recommend build, improve, experiment, buy, partner, defer, or reject.

## Core principles

- Evidence before recommendations.
- Separate verified findings from estimates and hypotheses.
- Never present planned experiments as completed.
- Use consistent conditions for comparisons.
- Do not assume an AI solution is necessary.
- Report uncertainty and limitations.
- Prioritize decision quality over report length.

## Typical outputs

Product overview, customer research, market opportunity, competitor comparison, technical feasibility, experiment plan, risk register, product requirements, MVP, unit economics, and 30/60/90-day roadmap.

## Notes

This framework is educational and must be adapted to the product, jurisdiction, and available evidence. It does not replace domain-specific professional testing or certification.

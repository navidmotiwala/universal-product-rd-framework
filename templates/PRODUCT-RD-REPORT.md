# Product R&D Decision Report

## 1. Executive decision
State the recommended action, evidence, confidence, costs, risks, and owner.

## 2. Product and customer
Scope, customer jobs, current product, user journey, observed pain points.

## 3. Market and competitors
TAM, SAM, SOM with source-backed formulas, growth, competitive positioning, substitutes.

## 4. Feasibility
Technical options, architecture, dependencies, privacy, security, regulation, safety.

## 5. Experiments
Completed tests and findings. Planned tests must be clearly labeled not executed.

## 6. Commercial model
Pricing, acquisition, retention, revenue, margins, scenarios, sensitivity.

## 7. Opportunities and decisions
Prioritized ideas with evidence, impact, effort, risk, and confidence.

## 8. Roadmap
30/60/90-day plan with owners, milestones, acceptance criteria, and rollback gates.

## 9. Contradictions and limitations
Evidence conflicts, untested assumptions, missing access, disconfirming evidence.

## 10. Appendix
Source register, raw calculations, protocols, artifacts, decision log, risk register.

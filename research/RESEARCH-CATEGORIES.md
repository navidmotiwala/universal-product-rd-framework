# Research Category Library

The full conversation framework grew to **275 numbered reference categories**. Many overlap. Use this document as a consolidated navigation index, not a claim that all 275 are mutually exclusive or mandatory.

## 1. Product discovery and user research
Product overview, evolution, feature inventory, user journeys, JTBD, problem-solution fit, user interviews, contextual inquiry, ethnography, diary studies, personas, segmentation, non-users, latent needs, customer mental models, adoption friction, behavioral research, accessibility, localization, customer co-creation.

## 2. Market and competitive intelligence
TAM, SAM, SOM, CAGR, demand forecasting, market saturation, competitor discovery, competitive teardown, product forensics, market white space, strategic positioning, competitor response, market entry, category creation, market diffusion, substitution, pricing, willingness to pay, price elasticity, market concentration, defensibility.

## 3. Product design and experimentation
Concept testing, prototype testing, usability testing, behavioral intervention testing, pilot programs, beta programs, hypotheses, A/B testing, causal inference, natural experiments, sequential experimentation, factorial design, statistical power, multiple-testing correction, measurement error, external validity, replication, independent review.

## 4. Engineering and technical feasibility
System architecture, architecture decision records, technical debt, technology readiness, design space exploration, build versus buy, interoperability, API contract testing, performance, scalability, reliability, chaos engineering, fault injection, concurrency, data migration integrity, observability, maintainability, security, privacy, versioning, decommissioning.

## 5. AI and emerging technologies
Model selection, routing, evaluation, calibration, hallucinations, bias, safety, prompt injection, retrieval quality, data contamination, benchmark saturation, drift, abstention, agent authorization, rollback, memory quality, multimodal consistency, inference optimization, model update compatibility, human oversight.

## 6. Hardware, manufacturing, and physical systems
Materials, component selection, BOM, supplier qualification, supply chain, design for manufacturing, assembly, tolerance, reliability engineering, FMEA, accelerated life testing, ergonomics, product safety, certification, repairability, disassembly, recycling, energy and resource efficiency.

## 7. Commercial and financial analysis
Pricing, packaging, monetization, CAC, LTV, margins, ARR, MRR, unit economics, channel economics, sales processes, enterprise procurement, billing, renewal, churn, customer success, cannibalization, portfolio optimization, cost of delay, real options, ROI, NPV, TCO.

## 8. Risk, governance, and compliance
Threat modeling, abuse economics, fraud, identity, authorization, credential management, cybersecurity lifecycle, SBOM, regulatory horizon scanning, data rights, residency, experiment ethics, research governance, intellectual property, liability, resilience, incident response, product shutdown.

## 9. Research science and knowledge management
Research question decomposition, literature mapping, patent landscape, novelty assessment, evidence triangulation, contradictory evidence, Bayesian updating, value of information, scientific uncertainty, meta-analysis, reproducibility, knowledge transfer, negative results, peer review, research artifact management, traceability.

## 10. Product operations and lifecycle
Requirements, PRD, acceptance criteria, MVP, roadmap, configuration management, verification and validation, field testing, operational readiness, release sequencing, supportability, change management, adoption, customer outcome verification, post-launch research, maintenance, upgrades, migration.

## 11. Research leadership and portfolio management
Research prioritization, resource allocation, portfolio balance, capacity forecasting, skill gaps, external collaboration, confidentiality, dependency scheduling, research milestone quality, research productivity, capability maturity, research-to-commercialization handoff.

## 12. Specialized product experiences
Offline-first design, cross-device continuity, real-time collaboration, notification effectiveness, search and recommendation quality, information architecture, user-generated content moderation, identity and reputation, device interoperability, physical-digital interactions.

## Selection procedure

- Start with the product decision and critical unknowns.
- Choose relevant modules above.
- Apply industry overlays.
- Create an evidence and experiment plan.
- Challenge assumptions and report gaps.
- Stop when further research is unlikely to change the decision.

For the full execution prompt, see [Universal R&D Master Prompt](../prompts/UNIVERSAL-RD-MASTER-PROMPT.md).

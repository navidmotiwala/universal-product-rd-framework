# Universal Product R&D Execution and Quality Control

Act as the research operations and quality assurance lead for [PRODUCT].

Create a requirements-to-evidence traceability matrix. For each research question identify owner, method, evidence, validation status, acceptance criteria, and decision impact.

Evaluate study design, sampling, reproducibility, source provenance, data quality, ethics, technical feasibility, statistical assumptions, and operational readiness.

Keep a decision log and research artifact register. Escalate missing critical evidence. Check that results reflect experiments actually executed. Review negative findings and failed hypotheses.

Before publication, audit claims, formulas, links, scope, duplicated analysis, conflicting results, and unsupported recommendations.

Deliver a quality checklist, evidence gaps, corrective actions, and final research readiness decision.

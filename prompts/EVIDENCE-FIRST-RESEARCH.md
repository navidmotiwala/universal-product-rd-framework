# Evidence Before Recommendations Prompt

Research [PRODUCT] and [QUESTION].

First list what is known, unknown, and assumed. Collect reliable sources and record URLs, dates, methodologies, and relevance. Triangulate material claims where practical.

Assign evidence strength and explain uncertainty. Separate observed product behavior, customer statements, market estimates, and technical inference.

Only after evidence review, generate recommendations. For each recommendation cite the supporting evidence, describe alternatives, identify what could disprove it, and propose the next test.

Do not fabricate data, references, customer interviews, or experimental outcomes.

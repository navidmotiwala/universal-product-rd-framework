# Independent Product R&D Audit Prompt

Act as an independent auditor reviewing the research for [PRODUCT].

Evaluate research scope, question selection, source quality, data lineage, calculations, experiment design, replication, contradictions, customer representation, technical claims, commercial assumptions, ethics, and recommendation strength.

Identify unsupported claims, missing decision-critical evidence, false precision, irrelevant coverage, and cases where conclusions exceed the evidence.

Classify each finding by severity, impact, corrective action, owner, and verification method.

Deliver an audit summary, evidence exceptions, research-quality scorecard with explicit criteria, and a go/no-go decision for relying on the report. Do not imply formal certification.

# Market Sizing and Commercial Validation Prompt

For [PRODUCT] in [GEOGRAPHY], estimate market opportunity and commercial feasibility for [YEAR / HORIZON].

Cover TAM, SAM, SOM, top-down and bottom-up estimates, CAGR, customer segments, ICP, JTBD, willingness to pay, pricing, elasticity, competitors, adoption, CAC, LTV, ARPU, recurring revenue where applicable, retention, churn, gross margin, contribution margin, payback, and ROI.

Define market boundaries, units, currency, geography, and time horizon. Show formulas and calculations. Avoid double counting. Distinguish total market demand from realistic obtainable revenue.

Use verified sources and dates. Where data is unavailable, label assumptions and present conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios with sensitivity analysis.

Deliver market-sizing tables, unit economics, key risks, evidence gaps, and validation experiments. Never invent market numbers.

# Quick Start Product R&D Prompt

Analyze [PRODUCT NAME OR URL] for [OBJECTIVE].

Identify the customer problem, target users, product capabilities, competitive alternatives, TAM/SAM/SOM if evidence allows, technical feasibility, pricing, growth, risks, and meaningful improvement opportunities.

Separate verified facts from assumptions. Cite credible sources. Do not invent testing or numbers. Select only relevant research areas.

Deliver: executive summary, five key findings, five critical unknowns, competitor comparison, three high-value experiments, prioritized recommendations, and next ten actions.

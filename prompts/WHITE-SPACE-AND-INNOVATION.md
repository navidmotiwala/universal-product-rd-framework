# White Space and Innovation Discovery Prompt

For [PRODUCT] in [MARKET], identify opportunities not adequately served by existing solutions.

Study non-users, workarounds, customer jobs, substitutes, underserved segments, accessibility constraints, switching costs, emerging technologies, and adjacent industries.

For each opportunity define the unmet job, evidence, target segment, existing alternatives, why the gap persists, proposed concept, differentiation, feasibility, cost, risks, adoption barriers, business model, and the smallest test that could invalidate the idea.

Compare incremental, adjacent, and breakthrough concepts. Use TRIZ, morphological analysis, and cross-industry analogies when appropriate. Avoid novelty for novelty's sake.

Deliver a ranked opportunity map, concept briefs, falsifiable hypotheses, and a research roadmap. Distinguish original ideas from established market facts.

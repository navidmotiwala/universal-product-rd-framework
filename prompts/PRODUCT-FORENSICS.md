# Deep Product Forensics Prompt

Investigate [PRODUCT] as a product researcher, engineer, and competitor analyst.

- Map all publicly observable features, workflows, states, errors, limits, and pricing.
- Reconstruct customer journeys from entry through onboarding, activation, repeat use, support, and cancellation.
- Inspect accessible documentation, public change logs, reviews, release history, and product behavior.
- Distinguish observed technical behavior from guesses about proprietary implementation.
- Compare direct competitors, substitutes, and open-source alternatives.
- Identify design decisions, trade-offs, constraints, performance bottlenecks, and likely customer friction.
- Record reproducible steps, test environment, evidence, date, and confidence for every observation.
- Identify hidden assumptions, contradictions, unmet needs, and improvement opportunities.
- Recommend experiments, fixes, redesigns, and new features ranked by value, effort, and risk.

Output: product map, feature matrix, journey analysis, forensic findings, evidence log, and prioritized R&D opportunities. Never claim access or tests that did not occur.

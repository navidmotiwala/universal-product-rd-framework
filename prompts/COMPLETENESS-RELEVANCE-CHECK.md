# Universal Product R&D Completeness and Relevance Check

Review the existing research report for [PRODUCT].

1. Classify the product and the actual decisions being made.
2. Identify relevant disciplines and product-specific requirements.
3. Map covered questions against the chosen research scope.
4. Identify missing critical questions, not merely missing headings.
5. Flag duplicated, irrelevant, or speculative sections.
6. Identify untested assumptions and contradictory findings.
7. Evaluate source quality, research dates, sample adequacy, and methodological limitations.
8. Check technical, customer, commercial, operational, security, and regulatory risks as applicable.
9. Rank remaining gaps by probability of changing the decision.
10. Recommend research or experiments to close important gaps.
11. State what cannot be validated with available access.
12. Identify decision owners and next actions.
13. Define stopping criteria.
14. Produce a concise coverage matrix and revised research plan.
15. Stop when further research has low expected decision value.

Do not expand the report merely to increase its length.

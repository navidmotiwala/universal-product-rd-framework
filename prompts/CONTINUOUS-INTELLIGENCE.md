# Continuous Product Intelligence Prompt

Design a repeatable research monitoring program for [PRODUCT] and its competitors.

Monitor only relevant public or authorized sources: product releases, documentation, prices, customer feedback, security notices, regulations, technology developments, distribution, and market changes.

For each signal define source, frequency, threshold, owner, importance, and potential product decision. Separate confirmed changes from rumors. Deduplicate repeated reports. Detect meaningful changes, not noise.

Deliver a monitoring inventory, change log template, alert criteria, monthly review format, and escalation rules. Do not claim ongoing monitoring has been activated unless an actual scheduled workflow exists.

# Contradictory Evidence and Assumption Challenge Prompt

Act as an independent skeptic reviewing research on [PRODUCT].

1. Extract every material claim, recommendation, and assumption.
2. Locate evidence supporting and contradicting each claim.
3. Examine source independence, selection bias, recency, confounders, and measurement quality.
4. Identify alternative explanations and counterfactuals.
5. Quantify uncertainty where possible without inventing precision.
6. Specify evidence that would reverse the current recommendation.
7. Separate factual disputes from differences in judgment.
8. Recommend the lowest-cost decisive validation step.

Output a claim-evidence matrix, contradictions, unresolved questions, confidence changes, and revised decisions. Never manufacture opposing evidence.
